How to review

  1. Press Start review, then open your match in Dota 2: Watch > Replays.
  2. Set the replay's vision to your team and the camera to your hero, so you only see what you saw in the game.
  3. Pause the replay whenever you want the coach's view of that moment.
Fair play. The coach only looks at your screen while Dota is playing a replay. In your own live games it never looks at your screen, and the moment a live match starts, the screen is off. Frames stay on your PC and are never saved.

# Map objectives in patch 7.41: what a beginner should hear

Roshan, the Tormentors, wisdom shrines and towers decide low-bracket games
more than kills do: Herald to Ancient teams win a fight and then go back to
farming. This note collects what changed in 7.41 and what the coach can say,
using only the game clock and the player's own GSI data (items, gold,
health). The coach never knows whether an objective is alive or taken, so
every line is "think about it", never "it is up now".

## What to tell a beginner, and when

- **14:00 and 21:00 shrines are worth fighting for.** With 200/500/800, the
  second shrine gives 2.5 times the first and the third 4 times. A Herald
  who ignored the 7:00 one because it was small should hear that the next
  ones are bigger: "Wisdom shrines in 20 seconds: this one is worth more,
  take it with a teammate." Clock only.
- **Standing on a shrine with an enemy now loses it.** Since the timer runs
  backwards when both teams are there, the side that wins the skirmish gets
  it; going alone to a contested shrine is a death, not a draw.
- **20:00 is a two-objective minute.** Tormentors appear at 20:00, the same
  minute the coach already says "think Roshan" (`[reminders.roshan]`). One
  line can cover both: "Tormentors are up and Roshan matters now: after a won
  fight, take one of them instead of farming." The shard goes to a poor
  teammate (usually a support), so it also helps supports.
- **The Tormentor is a team job.** It reflects damage and gains a stronger
  shield every minute in 7.41, so a lone carry at 25:00 loses health and
  time. Say "with three or four teammates", never "solo it".
- **Aegis in your own inventory means push now.** GSI shows `item_aegis` in
  the player's own items. The Aegis is a reason to take high ground with the
  whole team while it lasts, not to farm. This is the one Roshan line the
  coach can say knowing Roshan was just taken (by the player).
- **After 30:00 waves are stronger.** Losing a lane's wave to farm elsewhere
  costs a tower faster; "clear the wave before you farm" fits deaths and the
  25:00 check.

## Open checks

- Tier 1 neutral items: two patch-note summaries say 0:00, Liquipedia (read
  2026-10-31) said 5:00. If the Madstone counter can craft a tier 1 before
  5:00 in a real match, the coach's first neutral line comes too late and
  should move to the first few minutes (ROADMAP item 24).
- Roshan's drops changed a few times since 7.33; the coach should name only
  the Aegis, which every source agrees on.
